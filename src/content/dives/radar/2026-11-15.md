One of the most useful facts on the certification pages this cycle is a date, not a launch. The AWS Machine Learning Specialty exam stopped being offered on 31 March 2026. Microsoft's Azure AI Engineer Associate went on 30 June. If either was on your list, the list needs a second look.

This is the first Learning Radar, so it covers the current state rather than a delta. Every name, price and date below comes from the provider's own page, read on 15 November 2026. Where a page doesn't say, so do we.

## Certifications: what's moving

**AWS Certified Generative AI Developer – Professional (new).** 300 USD, 180 minutes, 75 questions (multiple choice or multiple response), at a Pearson VUE centre or online. No prerequisite certification, but AWS describes the candidate as having 2+ years building production applications plus one year of hands-on generative AI work. The page gives no launch date, so we can't say how long it has been open. Why it matters: it is the first AWS professional-level exam aimed at generative AI builders rather than classic ML. [Exam page](https://aws.amazon.com/certification/certified-generative-ai-developer-professional/). Its predecessor, Machine Learning – Specialty, is retired. AWS says holders keep an active certification for three years from the date earned. The page points to Machine Learning Engineer – Associate as an alternative, without calling it a formal replacement. The AWS hub page also shows the Machine Learning – Associate exam as "being updated". [Retirement notice](https://aws.amazon.com/certification/certified-machine-learning-specialty/).

**Microsoft Azure AI Engineer Associate, AI-102 (retired 30 June 2026).** Microsoft's retired-exams page lists it, along with AI-900 (30 June) and DP-100 (1 June). The Azure AI Engineer page lists skill areas that include implementing generative AI and agentic solutions, but names no successor exam, so we won't guess one. [Retired exams](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/credentials/retired-certification-exams).

**Google Cloud Professional Machine Learning Engineer (content updated).** 200 USD, two hours, 50 to 60 questions. The page says this version covers generative AI tasks, including Model Garden, Vertex AI Agent Builder and evaluating generative AI solutions. It recommends 3+ years of industry experience, 1+ on Google Cloud. For contrast, Google's Generative AI Leader exam (99 USD, 90 minutes) is aimed at any job role and is not an architect credential. [PMLE](https://cloud.google.com/learn/certification/machine-learning-engineer).

## Our pick this cycle

**Building AI Assistants with On-Device Memory** (Qdrant, about an hour).

It's the cheapest thing on this page and it lines up with the next two retrieval-heavy weeks. A certification is worth its price when a hiring process screens on it, which is more common in cloud-provider and enterprise roles than at frontier labs, where projects and writing count for more. If a cloud credential is on your path, the Google PMLE is the one whose content now matches current practice. If not, an hour spent building something beats 200 USD spent on an exam.
